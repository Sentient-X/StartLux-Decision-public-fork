# ABC-130k fixed 30-window visual audit — 2026-10-22

**Decision: the initial recovery-data gate fails for this pack.** All 30 fixed windows were visually inspected from top and both wrist cameras. The audit established 0 clear mistake-only events, 0 failed corrections and 0 successful restorations; 19 windows show ordinary coarse handling and 11 remain unknown. The target was at least 10 usable physical events. Failure-only would have qualified even without a later recovery; none was sufficiently clear at this observation budget.

This is a model-assisted visual audit, not human gold. Every row remains `training_eligible=false` and `human_label=null`. No labels were admitted to training; no paid labeling, Modal or other paid model job was started. The audit does not establish that these episodes contain no errors.

## What this exposes

Coarse transport/rotation is often visible for large rigid objects and fabric. Insertion fit, retention and contact are harder for transparent tubes, reflective staples, small fuses, black tools against black pouches, and contents inside opaque bags. Cloth regrasping or opening a box is not inherently a mistake. Different-object fruit/chocolate packing and different-slot fuse installation explain many repeated-action matches. These are qualitative morphology differences within this convenience pack, not morphology-level performance estimates.

The strongest ambiguous example is `e09-keyword`: the comb lies outside the pouch, is reacquired and placed inside. It was provisionally considered a possible correction during review; the final judgment is unknown because the initial release is concealed and could be deliberate reorientation. Likewise `e04-keyword` shows an orange fuse still on the table after an approach, but the missed-acquisition contact is not clear. Neither becomes a positive merely because its annotation says mistake. No ordinary-control row was promoted into a physical-event label.

The next decision is not to begin training a recovery detector from these mined hints. This audit supports annotation mining and frame extraction, but supplies zero qualified positive events for this gate. A separate, explicitly scoped higher-resolution/longer-context audit or human adjudication could revisit unknowns; it has not been performed here. This result does not compare StartLux, the frozen-encoder head or FLORA.
